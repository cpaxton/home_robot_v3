\section{Bonus: agentic verify-gate diagnostics}
\label{app:agentic_verify_gate}

Holdout-8 agentic wins (Section~\ref{sec:agentic_results}) used a short deterministic tour that often \emph{submitted without} SigLIP \texttt{PRESENT}.
On larger slices the same gate matters: image-space SigLIP cosine on Habitat RGB saturates near $0.12$--$0.14$ (offline calib on saved episode frames), well below the DynaMem voxel bar of $0.21$.
We therefore treat SigLIP as a \textbf{high-recall / high-false-positive proposal} for ``check here,'' not as a high-precision confirmer (Appendix~\ref{app:agentic_tools}; \texttt{docs/experiments/agentic\_scale.md}).
Image-space scores use three bands: \texttt{ABSENT}~$< 0.10$ (true-negative \emph{for this view}), \texttt{CANDIDATE}~$[0.10, 0.12)$, \texttt{PRESENT}~$\geq 0.12$.

\paragraph{Intended loop.}
(1)~Explore / inspect for candidates;
(2)~move in and score the \emph{new} view once;
(3)~assess---escalate or answer---never re-verify the same observation.

\paragraph{Hybrid confirmer and evidence policy.}
We retain MaskSigLIP for voxel grounding and as a cheap FP-leaning scorer, and add open-vocabulary OWLv2/YoloE and optional detector-crop$\rightarrow$SigLIP channels.
The policy is an explicit \textsc{Search}$\rightarrow$\textsc{Approach}$\rightarrow$\textsc{Verify}$\rightarrow$\textsc{Assess}$\rightarrow$\textsc{Replan}$\rightarrow$\textsc{Answer} loop.
It records channel-specific evidence and provenance, rejects stale-view verification, and separates target presence from answer sufficiency.
A scene-disjoint decision dataset stores only causal trace prefixes and view-level semantic-sensor visibility (mask area, bounding box, occlusion proxy, and range), rather than using target distance as visibility.

\paragraph{Preliminary offline bakeoff.}
On 24 saved verify views, mean per-view latency was 49.8\,ms for SigLIP, 32.4\,ms for SigLIP2, 60.6\,ms for OWLv2, and 234.6\,ms for YoloE.
The legacy bundles did not retain semantic masks, so this pass has no true view labels and must not be interpreted as detector precision/recall.
We selected OWLv2 for the subsequent labeled live probe based on this latency result; threshold and accuracy claims are deferred to scene-disjoint labeled data.

\paragraph{Status.}
The detector hybrid, typed evidence policy, graph information-gain ranking, and selective-risk reporting are wired into the H2H path.
Balanced-32 remains gated on a 4--8 episode probe with nonzero fused verified-answer rate and zero forced submits.
Numbers in Section~\ref{sec:agentic_results} should be read as classic vs.\ tool-loop \emph{efficiency} (holdout-8 letters + balanced-32 steps), not as proof that detector confirmation alone succeeded.
