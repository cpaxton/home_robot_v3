The companion media manifest records source runs, artifact paths, and assistance
labels. The gallery generator preserves original videos and writes asset hashes;
recorded images are not synthesized or interpolated. New manipulation recordings
also save public JSON tool outcomes and before/after camera views. Assisted
articulation uses simulator joint teleportation and is evaluated separately from
physical opening and placement.
