\subsection{Habitat HM-EQA (preliminary)}
\label{sec:hmeqa_results}

We report early Dynagraph numbers on HM-EQA in Habitat-Sim (\texttt{.venv-habitat/bin/emet-habitat}), using \texttt{Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct}, 20 planning iterations, navmesh navigation (\texttt{--habitat-perfect-nav}), graph frontier nodes, MCQ debiasing, and Dynagraph's SigLIP-grounded \texttt{CONFIRMED\_MEMORY} block.
Table~\ref{tab:hmeqa_vs_prior} situates our numbers against GraphEQA paper baselines and our own full-benchmark reproduction; all \texttt{emet} rows use \textbf{local} VLMs and partial HM3D semantics unless noted.

\noindent\textbf{Gap to prior art.}
Our largest comparable run (gemma-3-4b, 113 questions) trails GraphEQA-GPT-4o by $\sim$22 percentage points---consistent with weaker local VLMs, incomplete GT semantics ($\sim$37/113 scenes at last audit), and a different scene-graph stack (Appendix~\ref{app:habitat_eqa_parity}).
Dynagraph still beats our graph\_eqa baseline on matched slices (+3\,pp on letter-balanced 32) and reaches 50\% on a held-out random eight-question subset with Qwen3-VL-8B after June 2026 grounding fixes (Table~\ref{tab:hmeqa_preliminary}).
Full 113-question evaluation with the 8B model and current stack is \textbf{planned} (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).

These slices are \textbf{not} directly comparable to Table~\ref{tab:grapheqa_official_hmeqa} until semantics coverage and/or VLM tier align with the reference.

\textbf{Engineering fixes (June 2026).}
Before hold-out evaluation we repaired Habitat navigation (compass/yaw, navmesh pathing, frontier deprioritization), graph-memory query failures (\texttt{KeyError} in observation indexing), and HM-EQA prompt grounding: phrase-level relevant objects, SigLIP retention through \texttt{prepare\_dynagraph\_vram\_for\_eqa}, and location-MCQ directives for ``did you see \ldots\ anywhere?'' questions where the VLM previously answered \texttt{No} instead of a place letter.

\noindent\textbf{Representative episode (Q17).}
``Did you see the woven basket anywhere?'' (gold D) failed before phrase+location fixes: exploration reached the basket ($\sim$0.66\,m) but \texttt{CONFIRMED\_MEMORY} marked token fragments as absent and the VLM answered visibility \texttt{No}, yielding an empty graded letter.
After the fixes, the same question completes in 21 planning steps and 2 EQA iterations with \texttt{answer: D}, citing graph memory for the basket near the living-room armchairs despite the object being out of frame in attached images.

\noindent\textbf{Held-out random-8 (anti-overfit check).}
We draw eight paper questions with seed 20260627, two per gold letter A--D, excluding the tuning set $\{3,14,17\}$.
Dynagraph scores \textbf{4/8} (50\%) with mean 50.5 planning steps---correct on Q15, Q79, Q88, and Q104; incorrect on Q56, Q65, Q68, and Q105.
This is above random guessing (25\%) and shows the Q17 fix generalizes beyond the tuning slice, while remaining far from GraphEQA's API-VLM baselines on the full benchmark (Table~\ref{tab:hmeqa_vs_prior}).

\noindent\textbf{Next experiments.}
Priority sweeps: (1)~full 113-question dynagraph with Qwen3-VL-8B and the current fix stack; (2)~matched graph\_eqa baseline on the same VLM; (3)~semantics download + ablation; (4)~frontier-policy ablation (fluid vs.\ graph nodes); (5)~optional API-VLM upper bound on a fixed slice to isolate stack vs.\ model tier.
Operator checklist: \texttt{docs/experiments/habitat\_eqa\_results.md}.
