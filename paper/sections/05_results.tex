\subsection{Classic vs.\ agentic verify}
\label{sec:agentic_results}

We compare the classic Dynagraph planning loop to the agentic tool loop on the same Dynagraph H2H profile (Qwen3-VL-8B; \texttt{explore\_when\_uncovered=off} ablation; Section~\ref{sec:agentic_h2h}).
On the anti-overfit holdout-8 ($\{15,56,65,68,79,88,104,105\}$; agentic \textbf{router off}), agentic reaches \textbf{8/8} vs.\ classic \textbf{5/8}, with mean planning steps cut from 60.3 to 18.3 (Table~\ref{tab:agentic_h2h}; Figure~\ref{fig:hmeqa_agentic_h2h}).
Agentic recovers all three classic misses (Q56, Q65, Q104).
An earlier holdout-4 gate showed the same pattern (3/4$\rightarrow$4/4; 60$\rightarrow$19 steps).

\noindent\textbf{Coverage maps.}
Figure~\ref{fig:hmeqa_agentic_coverage} renders side-by-side episode overlays from per-arm debug bundles (\texttt{scripts/render\_hmeqa\_agentic\_coverage\_figure.py}).
Distinct \texttt{--debug-run-tag}s (or \texttt{OUT/bundles/\{arm\}\_qN} snapshots) are required so H2H arms do not overwrite \texttt{\textasciitilde/.cache/habitat\_eqa/episodes/}.

\noindent\textbf{Scale.}
On the letter-balanced 32, paper-router agentic reaches 16/32 (50\%) vs.\ classic 9/32 (28\%) with mean planning steps cut from 48.7 to 32.7 (Figure~\ref{fig:hmeqa_agentic_h2h_bal32}; cross-policy McNemar $p{\approx}0.09$).
The 16/32 agentic headline is stable across later agentic-only replications (11--16/32 over five July--August runs; the post-fix stack again reaches 16/32).
A prior matched router-off H2H was 11/32 vs.\ 9/32 (archived); an overnight replicate under that policy was 12/32 vs.\ 10/32.
Checked-in summaries: \texttt{paper/data/hmeqa\_agentic\_h2h/balanced32\_summary.json} (composite) and \texttt{balanced32\_overnight\_replicate.json}.
Appendix~\ref{app:agentic_verify_gate} documents the high-recall SigLIP role and the policy timeline.

\noindent\textbf{Robustness of the letter claims.}
Run-to-run variance on small slices is real: classic on the holdout-8 prefix $\{15,56,65,68\}$ scored between 25\% and 100\% across four independent August runs, and agentic under paper-router scored 5/8 on holdout-8 (Appendix~\ref{app:agentic_verify_gate}).
We therefore treat the \emph{mean planning step} reduction as the primary, statistically robust claim (Wilcoxon $p{\approx}2{\times}10^{-5}$ on balanced-32) and the letter gains on holdout-8 as the matched same-policy headline, rather than overfitting to any single draw.

\subsection{Habitat HM-EQA vs.\ prior art (preliminary)}
\label{sec:hmeqa_results}

Cross-environment harness tuning (\texttt{configs/benchmarks/dynagraph.yaml}) sets HM-EQA \texttt{dynagraph} to \texttt{unified\_eqa} (0.45\,m merge), MCQ debias off, and \texttt{explore\_when\_uncovered=conservative}; HM-EQA \texttt{static\_graph} uses profile \texttt{static\_graph} (merge/staleness off).
Tuned full-113 numbers are \textbf{pending}.

\noindent\textbf{Gap to prior art.}
Our largest comparable run (gemma-3-4b, 113 questions) trails GraphEQA-GPT-4o by $\sim$22 percentage points---consistent with weaker local VLMs, incomplete GT semantics ($\sim$37/113 scenes at last audit), and a different scene-graph stack (Appendix~\ref{app:habitat_eqa_parity}).
On matched slices Dynagraph is competitive with our \texttt{static\_graph} baseline (+3\,pp on letter-balanced 32 at 3B); holdout-8 after July nav favored \texttt{static\_graph} until harness tuning (Table~\ref{tab:hmeqa_preliminary}).
Full 113-question evaluation with the 8B model and current stack is \textbf{planned} (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).

These slices are \textbf{not} directly comparable to Table~\ref{tab:grapheqa_official_hmeqa} until semantics coverage and/or VLM tier align with the reference.

\textbf{Engineering fixes (June--July 2026).}
Before hold-out evaluation we repaired Habitat navigation (compass/yaw, navmesh pathing, frontier deprioritization), graph-memory query failures (\texttt{KeyError} in observation indexing), and HM-EQA prompt grounding: phrase-level relevant objects, SigLIP retention through \texttt{prepare\_dynagraph\_vram\_for\_eqa}, and location-MCQ directives for ``did you see \ldots\ anywhere?'' questions where the VLM previously answered \texttt{No} instead of a place letter.
In July 2026 we added Image-$N$ navigation waypoints (capture viewpoint or standoff toward object anchor, not raw centroid), navmesh trajectory execution with \texttt{already\_at\_goal} blocking, and frontier sorting by planar distance---reducing spin-in-place on search-style questions.
Held-out random-8 numbers below include the July nav stack (\texttt{postfix\_nav20260705\_larger}); \texttt{static\_graph} (logged as graph\_eqa) reached 7/8 while legacy dynagraph extras (debias, aggressive frontier) scored 3/8---motivating per-harness tuning.

\noindent\textbf{Representative episode (Q17).}
``Did you see the woven basket anywhere?'' (gold D) failed before phrase+location fixes: exploration reached the basket ($\sim$0.66\,m) but \texttt{CONFIRMED\_MEMORY} marked token fragments as absent and the VLM answered visibility \texttt{No}, yielding an empty graded letter.
After the fixes, the same question completes in 21 planning steps and 2 EQA iterations with \texttt{answer: D}, citing graph memory for the basket near the living-room armchairs despite the object being out of frame in attached images.
