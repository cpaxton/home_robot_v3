Controller safety evidence

Archived simulator evaluations. Cohorts remain separate: matching seeds alone do not establish matching models, controllers, or initial states. These results do not measure hardware safety or combined base–arm performance.

Rates show observed events / evaluated episodes and 95% Wilson intervals. Safety outcomes exclude failed initializations; task success includes them as failures. Missing or unsupported outcomes remain unknown. Zero observed failures is not proof of zero risk.

Intervals assume independent trials. Repeated seed/case identities and missing identities are flagged without deleting records; record counts are not independent-evidence counts.
